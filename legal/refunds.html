Refund & Returns Policy

Information about cancellations, returns and refunds.

Last updated: September 2026

1. General Policy

KYLO Agric Solution aims to supply customers with products that meet the agreed specifications and reasonable quality expectations.

If you believe there is a problem with an order, contact us as soon as reasonably possible.

2. Non-Perishable Products

Certain non-perishable products may be eligible for return where they are unused, undamaged and in a condition suitable for resale.

Eligibility will depend on the product and circumstances of the order.

3. Perishable Agricultural Products

Fresh produce and other perishable agricultural products may not be eligible for ordinary returns because of their nature.

Customers should inspect such products as soon as possible after collection or delivery and notify KYLO of any significant issue.

4. Livestock and Poultry

Livestock, poultry and live agricultural products require special handling and may have specific sale and return conditions.

Any concerns relating to live animals should be reported promptly so that the circumstances can be assessed.

5. Damaged or Incorrect Products

If an item is damaged, defective or materially different from the agreed product, contact KYLO with:

  • Order number
  • Description of the problem
  • Photographs where appropriate
  • Date of delivery or collection

6. Cancellations

Cancellation requests should be submitted as early as possible. Cancellation may not be possible after a product has been dispatched, collected, specially prepared or otherwise committed for fulfilment.

7. Refunds

Where a refund is approved, the method and timing of the refund may depend on the original payment method and payment provider.

Third-party payment providers may have their own processing times.

8. Custom or Special Orders

Specially sourced, customised or made-to-order products may have different cancellation and refund conditions. These conditions may be communicated before confirmation.
