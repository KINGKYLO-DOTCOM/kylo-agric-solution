Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to KYLO Agric Solution.

Last updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

KYLO Agric Solution ("KYLO", "we", "our" or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you provide when using our website, products, services and enquiry systems.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on how you use our website, we may collect:

  • Name and surname
  • Telephone number
  • WhatsApp contact information
  • Email address
  • Delivery or collection information
  • Account login information
  • Products or services you enquire about
  • Order and transaction information
  • Messages submitted through our website
  • Technical information such as browser and device information

3. How We Use Information

We may use information to:

  • Process enquiries and orders
  • Communicate with customers
  • Arrange delivery or collection
  • Provide agricultural products and services
  • Maintain customer accounts
  • Process payments
  • Improve our website and services
  • Provide customer support
  • Meet applicable legal and administrative requirements

4. Payments

Online payments may be processed through third-party payment providers. KYLO Agric Solution does not intend to store complete bank-card information on its own website servers.

Payment providers may process payment information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

5. Customer Accounts

If you create an account, information associated with your account may be stored in our database so that we can provide account functionality, order history and customer services.

6. Cookies

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies for functionality, security, analytics and improving the user experience.

See our Cookie Policy for more information.

7. Third-Party Services

Our website may use third-party services for hosting, authentication, databases, payment processing, communications, analytics or other business functions.

These providers may process information according to their own terms and privacy policies.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse or disclosure.

However, no internet-based system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Data Retention

We may retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, maintain business records, resolve disputes, comply with legal obligations and protect our legitimate business interests.

10. Your Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights concerning your personal information, including requesting access to, correction of or deletion of certain information.

Requests can be sent to: kyloagricsolutions@gmai.com

11. Children's Information

Our website is intended for general customers and businesses and is not specifically directed at children.

12. Policy Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, technology or legal requirements change. The updated version will be published on this page.
