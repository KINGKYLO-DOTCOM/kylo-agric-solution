Delivery Policy

How KYLO Agric Solution handles collection and delivery.

Last updated: September 2026

1. Delivery Options

KYLO Agric Solution provides two main fulfilment options:

  • Customer collection
  • KYLO-arranged delivery

2. Customer Collection

Customers may collect eligible orders from the agreed collection location.

Collection arrangements, timing and identification requirements may be communicated after an order is confirmed.

3. KYLO Delivery

KYLO may arrange delivery depending on the product, quantity and destination.

Delivery availability may vary depending on location and logistical requirements.

4. Delivery Charges

Delivery charges are calculated manually based on relevant factors such as delivery location, order size, product type and transportation requirements.

The applicable delivery charge will be communicated before fulfilment where it is not already included in the order.

5. Delivery Times

Delivery time depends on product availability, preparation requirements, destination, transport availability and other circumstances.

Any delivery time provided by KYLO is an estimate unless specifically confirmed otherwise.

6. Perishable Products

Fresh produce and other perishable goods may require special delivery arrangements. Customers should ensure that someone is available to receive such products.

7. Livestock and Poultry

Live animals require appropriate transportation and handling. Delivery arrangements for livestock and poultry may therefore differ from ordinary products.

8. Customer Responsibilities

Customers are responsible for providing accurate delivery information and ensuring reasonable access to the delivery location.

Delays caused by inaccurate information or inability to receive a delivery may result in additional arrangements or charges.

9. Delivery Problems

If your order arrives damaged, incomplete or materially different from what was agreed, contact KYLO as soon as possible.
